## Measurement scope

`powermetrics` reports estimated SoC subsystem power, not whole-device AC input. The numeric value
must not be used as the Mac mini's total wall draw. Apple specifies Mac mini power consumption as
measured at the wall and including power-supply/system losses. Accurate whole-device watts therefore
require an external wall meter or watt-reporting smart plug. No such integration is currently
configured or visible in this repository.

The earlier 8–15 W idle/light estimate is not a live measurement of this M204 and should not be
presented as measured data. Wait for the user to identify an external meter or Home Assistant entity
before implementing a wall-input power adapter.
