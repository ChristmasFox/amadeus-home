# Amadeus 1.9.5 image retry and safety chain

Date: 2026-10-05 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Change

- Classify image failures into safety/policy refusal, account unavailability,
  invalid request, transient provider unavailability, and unknown.
- Retry the native image task at most once, only for transient timeout, rate
  limit, connection, or upstream 5xx failures.
- Stop 9Router Combo fallback on a safety/policy refusal so the same request is
  never sent to another provider as a moderation bypass.
- Carry only the failure category into the typed lifecycle message. Localized
  Kurisu notices keep provider payloads private and guide safety failures toward
  non-sensitive reformulation.
